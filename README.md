# Awesome Sauna [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of open projects, open data, standards and reference material about sauna, banya and other sweat-bathing traditions.

Every link was opened and checked in November 2026. Commercial shops and affiliate pages are left out on purpose. Listing a project is not an endorsement, and nothing here is medical or electrical advice.

## Societies and associations

- [Finnish Sauna Society](https://www.sauna.fi/en/) - Looks after Finnish sauna culture; publishes sauna knowledge articles and runs the saunas at Vaskiniemi in Helsinki.
- [International Sauna Association](https://saunainternational.net/) - Umbrella body of national sauna societies; organizes the International Sauna Congress.
- [North American Sauna Society](https://www.saunasociety.org/) - Promotes traditional sauna bathing in North America.
- [British Sauna Society](https://www.britishsaunasociety.org.uk/) - Promotes sauna culture and good practice in the United Kingdom.
- [Deutscher Sauna-Bund](https://www.sauna-bund.de/) - The German sauna association, with guidance for public saunas (German).
- [Sauna from Finland](https://saunafromfinland.com/) - Finnish association and company network around sauna expertise.
- [Japan Sauna and Spa Association](https://www.sauna.or.jp/) - Industry association for sauna and spa facilities in Japan (Japanese).
- [Aufguss WM](https://www.aufguss-wm.com/) - Home of the Aufguss world championship, the competition for sauna masters.

## Heritage and culture

- [Sauna culture in Finland](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/sauna-culture-in-finland-01596) - UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, inscribed in 2020.
- [Smoke sauna tradition in Võromaa](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/smoke-sauna-tradition-in-voromaa-00951) - UNESCO Representative List entry for the Estonian smoke sauna, inscribed in 2014.
- [Savvusann](https://www.savvusann.ee/) - Site about the Võromaa smoke sauna tradition (Estonian).
- [Bare facts of the sauna](https://finland.fi/life-society/bare-facts-of-the-sauna/) - Plain introduction to sauna customs from thisisFINLAND, the country's official site.
- [Sauna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauna) - Wikipedia overview with history, types and customs by country.
- [Finnish sauna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_sauna) - Wikipedia on the Finnish tradition, including the smoke sauna.
- [Banya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banya_%28sauna%29) - Wikipedia on the Russian steam bath.
- [Hammam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammam) - Wikipedia on the steam baths of the Islamic world.
- [Jjimjilbang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jjimjilbang) - Wikipedia on the Korean bathhouse.
- [Sentō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sent%C5%8D) - Wikipedia on the Japanese public bath.
- [Sweat lodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweat_lodge) - Wikipedia on the ceremonial sweat lodges of Indigenous peoples of the Americas.
- [Temazcal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temazcal) - Wikipedia on the Mesoamerican sweat house.
- [Sauna whisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauna_whisk) - Wikipedia on the vihta, vasta and venik.

## Design and building

- [Trumpkin's Notes on Building a Sauna](https://localmile.org/trumpkins-notes-on-building-a-sauna/) - Long, opinionated guide to bench heights, ventilation and heater choice.
- [The Finnish Sauna Basics](https://saunologia.fi/in-english/) - English articles from Saunologia, a Finnish site about sauna design.
- [The Secrets of Finnish Sauna Design](https://saunologia.fi/book-project-secrets/) - Book by Lassi A. Liikkanen on heat, air quality and interior design; this is the author's page about it.
- [SaunaTimes](https://www.saunatimes.com/) - Long-running blog on building and using saunas in North America.
- [Sauna knowledge](https://www.sauna.fi/en/sauna-knowledge/) - Background articles from the Finnish Sauna Society.
- [Building an outside sauna from scratch](https://github.com/jmwislez/sauna) - Photo log and notes from one outdoor sauna build.
- [How do I select the correct heater power?](https://support.harvia.com/hc/en-gb/articles/22158995674524-How-do-I-select-the-correct-heater-power) - Harvia's sizing rules for room volume, glass, stone and log walls.
- [How to dimension a sauna heater](https://huum.eu/how-to-dimension-a-sauna-heater-find-the-proper-power-for-your-sauna/) - HUUM's version of the same calculation, with a worked example.

## Calculators and models

- [Harvia Sauna Calculator](https://www.harvia.com/en/sauna/saunas/sauna-calculator/) - Heater sizing from room dimensions and surfaces, by the manufacturer.
- [HUUM heater calculator](https://huum.eu/calculator/) - Heater sizing from room volume and uninsulated surfaces, by the manufacturer.
- [SaunaSim](https://saunasim.com/) - Web interface to a model of how a sauna heats up ([source](https://github.com/markhalonen/sauna-sim)).

## Standards and safety

- [IEC 60335-2-53](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68677) - International safety standard for electric sauna heating appliances and infrared emitting units (paid document).
- [UL 875](https://www.shopulstandards.com/ProductDetail.aspx?productId=UL875) - UL standard for electric dry-bath heaters, the one used for sauna heaters in the United States (paid document).
- [EN 15821](https://www.evs.ee/en/evs-en-15821-2010) - European standard for wood-fired sauna stoves: requirements and test methods (paid document; check its current status with your national body).
- [Saunas and your health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/saunas-and-your-health) - Harvard Health Publishing on sensible limits and who should be careful.
- [Cold Water Hazards and Safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/coldwater) - U.S. National Weather Service on cold shock, relevant to plunges and ice swimming.
- [The plunge into cold water comes with risks](https://www.heart.org/en/news/2022/12/09/youre-not-a-polar-bear-the-plunge-into-cold-water-comes-with-risks) - American Heart Association News on cold water and the heart.

## Smart home

Projects that read or control a sauna from home automation software. Most of them are unofficial and not affiliated with the heater makers.

### Home Assistant integrations

- [HUUM](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/huum/) - Built-in Home Assistant integration for HUUM sauna controllers.
- [Saunum](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/saunum/) - Built-in Home Assistant integration for Saunum sauna controllers.
- [ha-harvia-xenio-wifi](https://github.com/RubenHarms/ha-harvia-xenio-wifi) - Custom integration for Harvia heaters with the Xenio WiFi panel.
- [ha-harvia-sauna](https://github.com/WiesiDeluxe/ha-harvia-sauna) - Custom integration for Harvia heaters with Xenio WiFi and Fenix panels.
- [harvia-home-assistant-plugin](https://github.com/TommyJuuti/harvia-home-assistant-plugin) - Custom integration for Harvia Fenix and SaunaSensor devices through the MyHarvia cloud.
- [sauna-card](https://github.com/krissen/sauna-card) - Dashboard card to show and control Harvia heaters.
- [esphome-tylo](https://github.com/f-io/esphome-tylo) - ESPHome component for Tylö Helo heaters over RS-485.
- [home-assistant-tylo-sauna](https://github.com/skyer/home-assistant-tylo-sauna) - Custom integration for the Tylö Elite panel with local control.
- [sentio](https://github.com/astrandb/sentio) - Custom integration for Sentiotec sauna controllers.

### Libraries and APIs

- [pyhuum](https://github.com/frwickst/pyhuum) - Python library for HUUM saunas, used by the Home Assistant integration.
- [huum-js](https://github.com/johnx25bd/huum-js) - TypeScript library for HUUM saunas.
- [HUUM API notes](https://github.com/horemansp/HUUM) - Short description of the HUUM cloud API endpoints.
- [pysaunum](https://github.com/mettolen/pysaunum) - Python library for Saunum controllers over Modbus TCP.
- [ioBroker.huum-sauna](https://github.com/Chris-656/ioBroker.huum-sauna) - Adapter that connects HUUM saunas to ioBroker.
- [saunabar](https://github.com/khasinski/saunabar) - Menu bar app for macOS that monitors and controls a Saunum heater.

### DIY controllers

These projects switch mains-powered heaters. They are listed for study. A sauna heater is a fire and shock hazard: keep the certified safety cut-outs, follow your electrical code, and have a licensed electrician do the wiring.

- [Sauna-controller](https://github.com/killee/Sauna-controller) - ESP8266 and ESPHome controller with Home Assistant integration.
- [HarviaWiFi](https://github.com/SwiCago/HarviaWiFi) - WiFi control for a Harvia KIP heater.
- [SaunaKit](https://github.com/quicklywilliam/saunakit) - Remotely operated sauna build with a clear write-up of the design.
- [sauna-controller](https://github.com/alexlmiller/sauna-controller) - Locally controlled sauna based on ESPHome.
- [sauna-controler](https://github.com/Yimbo/sauna-controler) - ESPHome controller with a thermostat, a physical button and a display.
- [sauna-automation](https://github.com/bastiaanterhorst/sauna-automation) - Remote start, thermostat and push notifications with Home Assistant and a Raspberry Pi.
- [huum-controller](https://github.com/kpalang/huum-controller) - Local controller software for HUUM heaters.
- [ESPHome](https://esphome.io/) - The firmware framework most of these builds use.

## Open data

- [OpenStreetMap: leisure=sauna](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:leisure%3Dsauna) - How saunas are mapped in OpenStreetMap.
- [Taginfo: leisure=sauna](https://taginfo.openstreetmap.org/tags/leisure=sauna) - Live counts and a map of saunas tagged in OpenStreetMap.

- [Wikidata: sauna](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q57036) - The Wikidata item, a starting point for linked-data queries.
- [Wikimedia Commons: Saunas](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Saunas) - Freely licensed photos, sorted by country and type.
- [Sauna Ikitai](https://sauna-ikitai.com/) - Large community directory of sauna facilities in Japan (Japanese; a directory, not open data).

## Research

Peer-reviewed papers that are often cited. Read them as research, not as advice, and talk to your doctor about your own health.

- [Association Between Sauna Bathing and Fatal Cardiovascular and All-Cause Mortality Events](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2014.8187) - Laukkanen and others, JAMA Internal Medicine, 2015. Cohort study of Finnish men.
- [Cardiovascular and Other Health Benefits of Sauna Bathing: A Review of the Evidence](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mayocp.2018.04.008) - Laukkanen, Laukkanen and Kunutsor, Mayo Clinic Proceedings, 2018. Review article.
- [Clinical Effects of Regular Dry Sauna Bathing: A Systematic Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5941775/) - Hussain and Cohen, Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2018. Open access.
